OVERVIEW

A Lifestyle Surrounded by Comfort & Calm

Nestled in the serene surroundings of Agashi, Virar West, Shanti Heights is designed for those who seek a peaceful lifestyle without staying away from the city’s everyday conveniences. Surrounded by greenery and fresh open spaces, the project brings together thoughtful design, modern living, and a warm community atmosphere to create homes that feel truly special. Every corner of Shanti Heights reflects comfort, elegance, and the joy of living in a place where nature, connectivity, and lifestyle come together perfectly.
